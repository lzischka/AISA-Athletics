Autumn Season

The school year begins with energy and competition. The Autumn season launches AISA Athletics and sets the tone for the year.

Season Overview

Autumn Athletics at AISA

The Autumn season typically runs from late August through early November. With four sports in play, it is the most active season on the AISA athletics calendar and includes major conference tournament opportunities in NESAC, ISAC, and EAC.

📅

Season Dates

Late August – Early November

Soccer

Boys & Girls Programs

Soccer is the flagship Autumn sport at AISA. Boys and Girls soccer programs each field Varsity, JV, and Middle School teams. The Lions compete in ISAC league play throughout the season and send Varsity squads to NESAC and EAC championship tournaments.

Volleyball

Girls & Coed Programs

Volleyball at AISA is a high-energy program that competes across multiple levels. The Varsity girls team aims for NESAC and EAC tournament berths, while JV and Middle School teams build the pipeline through ISAC local competition.

Varsity Girls JV Girls MS Girls

Conferences: NESAC · ISAC · EAC
🏃

Cross Country

Boys & Girls Programs

Cross Country is an individual and team sport that tests endurance and mental toughness. AISA runners compete in ISAC invitational meets throughout the season, building fitness and camaraderie before a culminating end-of-season championship event.

Varsity Boys Varsity Girls MS Boys MS Girls

Conference: ISAC
🎾

Tennis

Boys & Girls Programs

Tennis offers individual and doubles competition for AISA student-athletes. The Varsity program competes in NESAC and EAC tournaments, while ISAC provides local match opportunities. Both boys and girls compete across singles, doubles, and mixed doubles formats.
