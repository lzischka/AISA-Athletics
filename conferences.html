International Competition

Our Conferences

AISA Lions compete in three prestigious international school athletic conferences, giving our student-athletes the opportunity to test themselves against peers from across the region and around the world.

Overview

Three Conferences. One Lion Pride.

Depending on the sport and team level, AISA participates in three different international school athletic conferences. Each conference has its own membership, geographic scope, and schedule of championship events.

🌐

NESAC

Near East South Asia Council of Overseas Schools – Athletic Conference

NESAC is a consortium of American-sponsored overseas schools in the Near East and South Asia region. The NESAC Athletic Conference brings together top-tier international schools for championship tournaments in a variety of sports, representing the highest level of competition available to AISA student-athletes.

Region Near East & South Asia
Level Varsity (Primary)
Tournament Format Annual Championship Tournaments
Travel Required Yes — International Travel

Sports Offered at NESAC

⚽ Boys Soccer ⚽ Girls Soccer 🏐 Volleyball 🏀 Boys Basketball 🏀 Girls Basketball 🎾 Tennis 🏊 Swimming 🏃 Track & Field ⚾ Baseball 🥎 Softball
🏫

ISAC

International Schools Athletic Conference

ISAC is a regional conference for international schools, typically focusing on local and sub-regional competition. ISAC provides a more accessible competitive environment for JV teams and Middle School programs, offering more frequent scheduling and reduced travel demands while still maintaining a high standard of competition.

Region Local / Sub-Regional
Level Varsity, JV & Middle School
Tournament Format Season League + Playoffs
Travel Required Local & Regional Travel

Sports Offered at ISAC

⚽ Soccer 🏐 Volleyball 🏀 Basketball 🏸 Badminton 🏊 Swimming 🏃 Cross Country 🏃 Track & Field ⚾ Baseball / Softball
🌏

EAC

East African Conference (of Overseas Schools)

EAC serves international schools across East Africa and connects AISA with a broader network of schools across the continent. The EAC provides championship tournament opportunities particularly in team sports, and participation reflects AISA's commitment to building continental connections through athletics.

Region East & West Africa
Level Varsity (Primary)
Tournament Format Annual Championship Events
Travel Required Yes — Continental Travel

Sports Offered at EAC

⚽ Boys Soccer ⚽ Girls Soccer 🏐 Volleyball 🏀 Basketball 🎾 Tennis 🏃 Track & Field 🏸 Badminton
