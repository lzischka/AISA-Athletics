Winter Season

When the pace of the school year hits its stride, Winter athletics bring the heat. The Lions compete indoors and push toward conference championships in their toughest season.

Season Overview

Winter Athletics at AISA

The Winter season runs from mid-November through late February. Basketball is the centerpiece of the season, with Swimming and Badminton rounding out a dynamic indoor athletics schedule for AISA student-athletes.

Basketball

Boys & Girls Programs

Basketball is the premier Winter sport at AISA. The Lions run robust Boys and Girls programs at all three competitive levels. Varsity teams compete for NESAC and EAC championship berths, while JV and MS teams gain experience through ISAC league play. Home games at AISA's gymnasium draw some of the biggest crowds of the school year.

Swimming

Boys & Girls Programs

The AISA Lions swimming program offers both individual event and relay competition. Swimmers train daily and compete in ISAC invitational meets, with top performers qualifying for the NESAC Swimming Championships. The program emphasizes personal best times and continuous improvement.

Conferences: NESAC · ISAC

Badminton

Boys & Girls Programs

Badminton is a fast-growing sport at AISA and a cornerstone of the ISAC and EAC conference schedules. Athletes compete in men's singles, women's singles, doubles, and mixed doubles formats. The program is open to all skill levels at the JV level, with Varsity selection based on team trials.
