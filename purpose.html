Mission & Program

Purpose of Athletics at AISA

We believe sport is a vehicle for developing responsible, resilient, and compassionate leaders. Every team. Every season. Every student-athlete.

Our Mission

Why Athletics Matters at AISA

The AISA Athletics program exists to complement the academic mission of the school — teaching life skills through the unique environment of competitive sport.

🤝

Teamwork

Learning to collaborate, communicate, and trust others both on and off the field.

💪

Resilience

Embracing setbacks as opportunities to grow stronger and more determined.

🏆

Excellence

Striving for the highest standard in every practice, game, and decision.

🌍

Global Citizenship

Competing internationally builds cross-cultural respect and understanding.

🎓

Student-First

Academics always come first. Athletes are students who play sports, not the other way around.

⚖️

Sportsmanship

Competing with integrity, respecting opponents, officials, and the game itself.

Philosophy

Our Guiding Philosophy

📚

Academics First

Student-athletes at AISA are expected to maintain strong academic standing. Participation in athletics is a privilege contingent on meeting academic requirements. Our coaches and AD work closely with teachers to monitor progress and provide support.

🌱

Development Over Winning

While we compete to win, our primary goal is the holistic development of every student-athlete. Coaches are trained to prioritize growth, playing time at developmental levels, and a positive experience that keeps students engaged in sport.

🌐

International Perspective

As a member of three conferences — ISAC (Abu Dhabi local), EAC (UAE-wide Emirates Athletics Conference), and NESAC (Near East Schools Activities Conference spanning Kuwait, Lebanon, and Egypt) — AISA students compete against peers from across the region, building a broad perspective through sport.

Team Structure

Team Levels at AISA

AISA Athletics organizes teams into three competitive levels, each designed to meet student-athletes where they are in their development and provide appropriate levels of challenge and playing time.

🏆

Varsity

The highest level of competition at AISA. Varsity teams represent the school in all conference tournaments (NESAC, ISAC, EAC) and are composed of the school's most skilled and experienced student-athletes. Varsity athletes are held to the highest standards of performance, commitment, and sportsmanship.


Grades 9–12 · Conference-eligible · Travel required
📈

Junior Varsity (JV)

JV teams provide a bridge between Middle School and Varsity competition. JV emphasizes skill development, increased playing time for all athletes, and preparation for the demands of Varsity competition. JV athletes play a full schedule of inter-school games, primarily within the local region.


Grades 9–10 · Local schedule · Development focus
🌱

Middle School (MS)

Middle School athletics are designed to introduce younger students to the joy of competitive sport. The emphasis at the MS level is on participation, fundamentals, sportsmanship, and fun. MS teams compete in local ISAC middle school divisions and select regional tournaments.


Grades 6–8 · Local & regional play · Participation-first
Eligibility

Participation Requirements

All AISA student-athletes must meet the following requirements to participate in the athletics program each season.

📋

Academic Standing

Athletes must maintain a minimum GPA set by AISA administration. Students on academic probation may be restricted from travel or competition until standards are met.

🩺

Physical & Medical Clearance

A current annual physical examination is required before the first practice of each school year. Medical clearance forms must be on file with the Athletics Office.

✍️

Parent / Guardian Consent

Signed consent forms are required from a parent or guardian for each season. Travel permission slips are additionally required for any away games or tournaments.

💳

Athletics Fee

A per-season participation fee covers uniforms, equipment, and transportation. Financial assistance is available — contact the Athletics Office for details.
