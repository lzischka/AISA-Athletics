Spring Season

The final stretch of the school year brings championship energy to the track, diamond, and course as Lions athletes close out the year in style.

Season Overview

Spring Athletics at AISA

The Spring season runs from early March through May and serves as the culmination of the athletics year. Track & Field anchors the season alongside Baseball, Softball, and Golf — offering student-athletes a wide range of competitive experiences as the school year winds down.

Track & Field

Boys & Girls Programs

Track & Field is the largest Spring sport at AISA, encompassing sprints, distance events, hurdles, relays, jumps, and throws. Athletes compete in ISAC invitationals throughout the season, with the top performers earning spots at the NESAC and EAC Track & Field Championships — the marquee event of the AISA Spring sports calendar.

Baseball

Boys Program

AISA Baseball is a competitive program with a proud tradition. The Lions squad plays a full ISAC season schedule against local international school opponents and sends its Varsity team to the NESAC Baseball Championships each Spring. The program develops both pitchers and position players through structured practice and game experience.

Varsity Boys JV Boys MS Boys

Conferences: NESAC · ISAC
🥎

Softball

Girls Program

The AISA Lions Softball program provides girls with a high-quality competitive experience from the diamond. Varsity competes at NESAC alongside Baseball, while ISAC provides the platform for JV and Middle School development. The program emphasizes hitting, fielding, and team chemistry throughout the season.

Varsity Girls JV Girls MS Girls

Conferences: NESAC · ISAC
⛳

Golf

Boys & Girls Programs

Golf at AISA provides a unique competitive and developmental opportunity. Student-athletes compete in stroke-play and match-play formats at local courses throughout the ISAC schedule. Golf emphasizes individual skill, mental discipline, and the highest standards of sportsmanship and etiquette. An ideal sport for beginners and experienced players alike.
